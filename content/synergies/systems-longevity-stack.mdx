> **TL;DR** — A full-spectrum protocol that layers seven mechanistic modules — redox/glutathione, NAD⁺/sirtuins, autophagy, NRF2/detox, senolytic, neurotrophic, and cardiovascular/ECM — so no single hallmark of aging is left uncovered. The individual legs carry Tier A/B human data; the **combined stack is graded Tier B** because no trial has tested all seven modules together. This is a build-up target, not a day-one purchase. Track GSH, hs-CRP, and NAD⁺ across 24 weeks and add modules one at a time.

## 1. Why this stack exists

Most "longevity stacks" pile compounds onto **one** pathway — five antioxidants, three NAD⁺ boosters — and leave whole hallmarks untouched. Aging is not one broken system; it is a network of interacting failures. A systems-biology stack assigns each compound to a **module** and asks a single question of the whole: *is any major hallmark uncovered?*

| Module | Anchor compounds (graded) | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Redox / glutathione core** | [GlyNAC](/library/compounds/glynac), [R-ALA](/library/compounds/rala), [astaxanthin](/library/compounds/astaxanthin) | Rebuild the master antioxidant (GSH); recycle it; protect lipid membranes |
| **NAD⁺ / sirtuin axis** | [NMN](/library/compounds/nmn), [resveratrol](/library/compounds/resveratrol), [pterostilbene](/library/compounds/pterostilbene), [PQQ](/library/compounds/pqq) | Restore NAD⁺; activate SIRT1/3, AMPK, PGC-1α; drive mitochondrial biogenesis |
| **Autophagy / proteostasis** | [spermidine](/library/compounds/spermidine) | Induce autophagy and mitophagy — the cellular cleanup no antioxidant provides |
| **NRF2 / detoxification** | [sulforaphane](/library/compounds/sulforaphane), [curcumin](/library/compounds/curcumin) | Switch on 200+ cytoprotective genes and phase-II detox enzymes |
| **Senolytic / senomorphic** | [fisetin](/library/compounds/fisetin), [quercetin](/library/compounds/quercetin), [luteolin](/library/compounds/luteolin) | Clear or quiet senescent cells; dampen the SASP inflammatory signal |
| **Neurotrophic** | [PQQ](/library/compounds/pqq), [omega-3](/library/compounds/omega3) | Support synaptic bioenergetics and neuronal membranes (Lion's Mane sits outside the graded catalog) |
| **Cardiovascular / bone / ECM** | [omega-3](/library/compounds/omega3), [vitamin D3](/library/compounds/vitamin-d3), [vitamin K2](/library/compounds/vitamin-k2), [Ca-AKG](/library/compounds/cakg), [CoQ10](/library/compounds/coq10), [hyaluronic acid](/library/compounds/hyaluronic-acid) | Endothelial function, calcium routing, and connective-tissue maintenance |

**Gap analysis — what a narrower stack misses:**

- Antioxidants without **autophagy** → oxidized proteins are neutralized but never *cleared*; damaged mitochondria accumulate.
- NAD⁺ boosters without **NRF2 induction** → sirtuins run, but the antioxidant gene battery stays under-expressed.
- Everything without a **senolytic** leg → SASP-driven inflammation keeps re-igniting the fires the rest of the stack puts out.

## 2. Mechanistic synergy map

```
Aging drivers: ROS · NAD⁺ decline · senescent burden · proteostasis loss
        │
[Redox core]  GlyNAC → GSH pool · R-ALA recycles GSSG→GSH · astaxanthin guards membranes
        │
[NRF2 switch] Sulforaphane + curcumin → NRF2 → GCLC, GCLM, NQO1, HO-1 (make more GSH)
        │
[NAD⁺ axis]   NMN → NAD⁺ → SIRT1/3 + PARP · resveratrol/pterostilbene activate SIRT1 · PQQ + PGC-1α → biogenesis
        │
[Autophagy]   Spermidine → autophagy/mitophagy (clears what redox only neutralizes)
        │
[Senolytic]   Fisetin + quercetin → clear/quiet senescent cells → ↓ SASP
        │
[Structural]  Omega-3 + D3/K2 + Ca-AKG + CoQ10 → vascular, bone, ECM maintenance
        ▼
Multi-hallmark coverage → slower functional decline
```

**Hallmarks addressed:** genomic instability · mitochondrial dysfunction · loss of proteostasis · disabled autophagy · cellular senescence · deregulated nutrient sensing · chronic inflammation · (epigenetic alterations, mechanistically). The design goal is **breadth with low redundancy** — each module owns a hallmark the others do not.

:::evidence tier="B"
Every module rests on Tier A/B human or mechanistic data, but **no trial has tested this seven-module combination**. TNiC grades the *stack architecture* Tier B: mechanistically strong and low-redundancy, with combination efficacy inferred rather than directly measured.
:::

**On epigenetic clocks:** supplement-only protocols are **not** yet proven to reverse epigenetic age in large controlled trials — diet, exercise, and pharmacologic regimens dominate the existing clock-reversal evidence, and the clocks themselves carry real technical noise. Treat any "biological age" claim for this stack as a mechanistic hypothesis to *test on yourself*, not an established result.

:::limitations title="Where the evidence is limited"
- **No trial tests the whole stack.** Every claim here is assembled from single-compound or single-module studies; the seven-module combination has never been tested end-to-end. Combined effects are inferred, not measured.
- **Biological-age reversal is unproven for supplement-only protocols.** The strongest epigenetic-clock-reversal data comes from diet, exercise, and pharmacology — not stacks like this one.
- **Several legs lean on preclinical or pilot data.** Fisetin's lifespan data is from aged mice (PMID 30279143); spermidine's cognitive human evidence is a pilot (PMID 33932338); astaxanthin and PQQ rest largely on mechanism. These are Tier B/C signals, not established human efficacy.
- **Individual response varies.** Your baseline labs and the personal-results log below matter more than any population average.
:::

## 5. Daily choreography

Sequencing matters more than it looks: substrate before signal, fat-soluble with food, senolytics pulsed rather than daily.

| Window | Module | Compounds | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wake (fasted) | Redox + NRF2 | GlyNAC, sulforaphane | Empty stomach; substrate first, then NRF2 induction |
| +30 min / breakfast | NAD⁺ + structural | NMN, Ca-AKG, R-ALA, omega-3, D3/K2, CoQ10 | R-ALA and fat-soluble vitamins with dietary fat |
| Midday | Autophagy | Spermidine | Away from high-protein meals where practical |
| Evening | Sirtuin | Resveratrol, pterostilbene | Fat-containing meal aids absorption; aligns with circadian SIRT1 |
| **Pulsed** (not daily) | Senolytic | Fisetin (± quercetin) | 2–3 consecutive days per month — the "hit-and-run" senolytic pattern, not continuous dosing |

**Why the senolytic leg is pulsed:** senescent-cell clearance in the published protocols uses short, intermittent high-dose windows, not daily microdosing. Running fisetin every day is neither how the mouse data was generated nor necessary.

:::practical title="Build order (do not start all seven modules at once)"
1. **Weeks 1–2:** Redox core (GlyNAC) alone — establish GI tolerance and a GSH baseline.
2. **Weeks 3–4:** Add the NRF2 leg (sulforaphane), then the NAD⁺ leg (NMN).
3. **Weeks 5–6:** Add structural (omega-3, D3/K2, Ca-AKG) and autophagy (spermidine).
4. **Week 8+:** Add the pulsed senolytic leg **only after** a clean tolerance record and updated labs.
5. **Pair with** the [GlyNAC deep-dive](/library/compounds/glynac) and [spermidine deep-dive](/library/compounds/spermidine) for single-leg detail.
:::

## 7. Red flags and contraindications

:::redflag title="Hard stops — do not run this stack without clearance"
- **Active chemotherapy or radiation** — high-dose antioxidant + senolytic legs may interfere with treatment intent
- **Anticoagulants / antiplatelet therapy** — omega-3, resveratrol, quercetin, and fisetin each affect bleeding risk; combined they compound it
- **Warfarin + vitamin K2** — K2 antagonizes warfarin dosing; do not add without INR management
- **Immunosuppression** — senolytic and NRF2 legs are unstudied in this context
- **Pregnancy or breastfeeding** — the combined stack is untested and not appropriate
:::

:::warning title="Soft cautions"
Introduce one module at a time — a seven-module stack makes any adverse reaction impossible to source if started all at once. Sulforaphane and Ca-AKG can cause mild GI upset; take with a small meal. R-ALA has a sulfur taste and may be split AM/PM. Verify third-party purity (COAs) on NMN, spermidine, and fisetin — market quality varies widely. Lion's Mane, collagen, silica, and biotin appear in many commercial versions of this stack but sit **outside TNiC's evidence-graded catalog**; they are not required for the mechanistic coverage above.
:::

More compounds means more interaction surface area and more cost; add them only when a specific hallmark or biomarker is still unaddressed.
