## 1. What Dasatinib does

Dasatinib is best understood through **senolytic tyrosine-kinase inhibition**. Dasatinib plus quercetin has early human senolytic studies, but dasatinib is a potent leukemia drug with serious hematologic and infectious risks and no role in self-experimentation. The evidence base below is anchored to PubMed records returned for this intervention (PMID 31542391, PMID 32686219, PMID 36857968); none proves that the compound extends human lifespan.

**Primary hallmarks targeted:** senescence · inflammation

:::evidence tier="C"
The rating reflects the gap between pathway or condition-specific outcomes and direct longevity evidence. A positive surrogate, symptom, or disease trial should not be promoted into a lifespan claim.
:::

## 6. Safety and red flags

:::redflag title="Do not confuse availability with safety"
This intervention is prescription-only or experimental. Do not source, dose, or combine it without an appropriately qualified clinician.

Evidence in one population does not establish safety in pregnancy, organ impairment, or polypharmacy. Product quality and dose accuracy matter.
:::
