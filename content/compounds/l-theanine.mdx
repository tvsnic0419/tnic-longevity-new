> **TL;DR** — L-theanine is an amino acid from tea leaves with solid human EEG data showing it increases alpha-wave activity (a marker of relaxed, alert wakefulness) without sedation. Its best-replicated real-world use is **paired with caffeine**, where it smooths caffeine's jitteriness while preserving its focus benefit. Tier B; 100–200 mg, often 1:1 or 2:1 with caffeine.

## 1. What L-theanine does

L-theanine is a non-protein amino acid found almost exclusively in tea (*Camellia sinensis*) that crosses the blood-brain barrier and measurably increases **alpha-wave activity** on EEG — a brainwave pattern associated with a relaxed-but-alert state, distinct from the drowsiness pattern sedatives produce. It's one of the more mechanistically clean nootropic/calming compounds: the EEG effect is well replicated, and unlike many "calming" supplements, it doesn't reliably cause drowsiness at typical doses.

**Primary hallmarks targeted:** [Altered intercellular communication](/library/altered-intercellular-communication) (neurotransmission, stress response)

:::evidence tier="B"
Multiple human RCTs using EEG confirm L-theanine (100–200 mg) increases alpha-wave activity within about 40 minutes, and several trials show reduced subjective stress/anxiety under cognitively demanding tasks. The best-replicated applied finding is the **caffeine + L-theanine combination**: paired dosing (commonly 1:1 or 2:1 theanine:caffeine) improves attention and reduces caffeine's jitteriness/anxiety side effects compared to caffeine alone, across several controlled trials. Tier B: a real, mechanistically clean effect with solid applied human data.
:::

:::practical title="The caffeine pairing is the best-evidenced use"
If you already drink coffee, adding **100–200 mg L-theanine per cup of coffee** (roughly matching a 1:1 to 2:1 theanine:caffeine ratio) is the single most replicated, practical application — smoother focus with less jitteriness and less of the caffeine crash.
:::

## 4. Dosing protocol

- **Dose:** 100–200 mg, alone or paired with caffeine (coffee/tea) at roughly a 1:1 to 2:1 theanine:caffeine ratio.
- **Timing:** with or shortly after caffeine intake for the pairing effect; standalone doses can be taken any time calm focus or evening relaxation is the goal.
- **Duration:** effects are acute (same-day), not cumulative — no loading period needed.

## 6. Safety, red flags, and contraindications

- **Excellent safety record** — L-theanine is present in tea at doses humans have consumed for centuries; supplemental doses are well tolerated.
- **Mild blood-pressure-lowering effect** — generally beneficial, but note if combining with antihypertensive medication.
- **No significant interaction profile** at typical doses.

:::redflag title="Coordinate before starting if any apply"
- **On antihypertensive medication** — monitor for additive blood-pressure lowering
:::
