> **TL;DR** — L-theanine is the amino acid in green tea that produces calm, focused alertness without sedation. Human EEG and cognitive studies show it raises alpha-wave activity and, paired with caffeine, sharpens attention while smoothing the jitter. A clean, fast-acting, extremely safe nootropic. 100–200 mg, on its own or with caffeine.

## 1. What L-theanine does (and why it matters)

Chronic stress and poor attention are quietly corrosive to healthspan — they drive cortisol, disrupt sleep, and erode cognition. L-theanine is one of the few supplements with a genuinely reliable acute effect here: within 30–40 minutes it shifts the brain toward an alert-but-relaxed state, measured directly as increased alpha-wave power. It is the reason a cup of green tea feels calmer than the same caffeine from coffee.

**Primary hallmarks targeted:** Altered intercellular communication · Chronic inflammation

## 2. Mechanism

L-theanine crosses the blood–brain barrier and modulates glutamate and GABA signaling, raises alpha oscillations, and dampens the sympathetic stress response. It blunts the vasoconstriction and jitter of caffeine while preserving — even enhancing — its attentional benefit.

## 3. Evidence

Controlled human studies (Gomez-Ramirez, Kimura, and others) show increased alpha activity, reduced subjective and physiological stress, and improved attention and reaction time when combined with caffeine. Effects are acute and consistent; it is one of the more replicable nootropic findings.

## 4. Dosing

100–200 mg as needed. With caffeine, a ~2:1 theanine-to-caffeine ratio (e.g., 200 mg theanine + 100 mg caffeine) is the classic focus pairing. Also used 200 mg in the evening to wind down.

## 5. Monitoring

Subjective — track focus, calm, and sleep quality. No lab monitoring needed.

## 6. Safety & red flags

Exceptionally safe with a long history of dietary use; no meaningful toxicity or dependence. May gently lower blood pressure, so note it if you are already on antihypertensives.

## 7. Synergies & stack integration

The canonical partner for **caffeine** (focus without jitter). Complements **magnesium** and **glycine** in an evening calm/sleep stack, and any cognition stack alongside **citicoline** or **lion's mane**.
