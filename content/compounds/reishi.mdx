> **TL;DR** — Reishi (Ganoderma lucidum) is the classical "spirit mushroom" of TCM. Human RCTs show immunomodulation via β-glucans (mixed cancer-adjunct data), small sleep-quality signal, and cardiovascular/glycemic biomarker changes. Larger meta-analyses find effect sizes small and inconsistent. Standard dose 1.5–3 g/day dual-extract (hot water + alcohol) to capture both β-glucans and triterpenoids. Real drug-interaction risks with anticoagulants and immunosuppressants.

## 1. What reishi does (and why it matters)

Reishi contains two chemically distinct fractions:
- **β-glucans** (water-soluble polysaccharides) — bind Dectin-1 and TLR2 on macrophages/dendritic cells, activating innate immune signaling.
- **Triterpenoids / ganoderic acids** (alcohol-soluble) — inhibit HMG-CoA reductase (statin-like), modulate 5α-reductase, and have preclinical anti-tumor effects.

A "dual extract" preparation captures both. Single-solvent extracts miss half the pharmacology — this is a key quality issue with cheap products.

**Primary hallmarks targeted:** Altered intercellular communication (immune signaling) · Chronic inflammation · Deregulated nutrient sensing

:::evidence tier="C"
Jin et al. (2016, PMID 27272893) Cochrane review of 5 RCTs in cancer patients — no evidence Ganoderma alone improves survival, but combined with chemotherapy improved tumor response in some studies. Chu et al. (2012, PMID 22995673) — 26 healthy adults, reishi 1.44 g × 4 weeks — reduced total cholesterol modestly. Wachtel-Galor 2004 — small (18 subjects) trial found no effect on standard biomarkers over 4 weeks. Human sleep evidence is limited to small open-label reports, not RCTs.
:::

## 2. Mechanism

| Pathway | Mechanism | Hallmark link |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dectin-1 / TLR2 | β-glucans activate innate immunity | Communication |
| NK cell activity | Increased in cancer-adjunct trials | Communication |
| HMG-CoA reductase inhibition | Triterpenoids (weak, statin-like) | Nutrient sensing |
| NF-κB modulation | Reduced inflammatory cytokines preclinically | Inflammation |
| GABAergic (proposed) | Sleep effect hypothesis; mechanism unclear | Communication |

:::practical title="When reishi is worth trying"
Reishi is worth thought when:

- You want an evidence-informed mushroom in an immune-support stack
- Physician-supervised cancer adjunctive care (not primary treatment)
- Chronic sleep disturbance with no other identified cause (small evidence)

**Skip or defer** if you take warfarin (real bleeding-time effect), immunosuppressants, or expect large results — RCTs show modest, inconsistent effects.
:::

## 3. Evidence summary

| Study | Design | N | Duration | Key outcomes | Tier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Jin 2016 Cochrane (PMID 27272893) | Meta-analysis | 5 RCTs | Various | No survival benefit alone; ↑ response with chemo | C |
| Chu 2012 (PMID 22995673) | RCT | 26 | 4 wk | ↓ Total cholesterol modestly | C |
| Klupp 2016 meta (PMID 26914009) | Meta of RCTs (diabetes) | 5 RCTs | 12–16 wk | No significant HbA1c or fasting glucose reduction | C |
| Traditional use | — | — | — | Millennia of use as adaptogen and immune support | — |

**Consensus:** Tier C. Genuine mechanism, mixed clinical signal, small studies. No longevity outcome data.

:::warning title="Where reishi disappoints"
- **Marketing runs far ahead of evidence.** RCTs are small and effect sizes modest.
- **Product quality varies wildly.** Whole mushroom vs mycelium-on-grain vs dual-extract vs single-solvent — hugely different active-compound profiles.
- **Real bleeding-time effect** — case reports of increased bleeding, especially with anticoagulants.
:::

## 6. Safety, red flags, and contraindications

- **Well tolerated** at typical doses; GI upset, dry mouth occasional.
- **Bleeding-time increase** — clinically meaningful with anticoagulants.

:::redflag title="Do not self-start without clearance"
- **On warfarin, DOACs, antiplatelets** — additive bleeding risk.
- **On immunosuppressants (post-transplant, autoimmune)** — β-glucans oppose immunosuppression.
- **Active bleeding disorder or scheduled surgery** — discontinue 2 weeks before surgery.
- **Pregnancy** — insufficient safety data.
:::
