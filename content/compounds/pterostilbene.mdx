> **TL;DR** — Pterostilbene is a methylated resveratrol analog with roughly 4× human oral bioavailability. Tier B: Kapetanovic 2011 confirmed safety and superior PK; longevity outcomes rely on SIRT1/AMPK mechanism shared with resveratrol. 50–150 mg PM with NMN AM; compare at /library/compare/resveratrol-vs-pterostilbene.

## 1. What pterostilbene does (and why it matters)

Trans-resveratrol's limitation is rapid glucuronidation. Pterostilbene's dimethyl structure slows clearance, achieving higher plasma exposure at lower doses — the same SIRT1 activation thesis with better pharmacokinetics.

**Primary hallmarks targeted:** [Mitochondrial dysfunction](/library/mitochondrial-dysfunction) · [Epigenetic alterations](/library/epigenetic-alterations) · [Chronic inflammation](/library/chronic-inflammation)

## 3. Evidence summary

| Study | Design | Key outcomes | Tier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kapetanovic 2011 (PMID 21749330) | Human PK/safety | 350 mg/day tolerated; higher exposure vs resveratrol | B |
| Animal mechanism literature (no single anchor study) | Preclinical | SIRT1/metabolic markers analogous to resveratrol | C |

**Consensus:** Bioavailability advantage is human-confirmed; longevity outcomes remain mechanistic extrapolation — same Tier B posture as resveratrol.

## 4. Dosing protocol

- **Dose:** 50–150 mg pterostilbene
- **Timing:** PM with fat-containing meal (pairs with AM NMN)
- **Avoid:** Duplicating both resveratrol and pterostilbene without reason
- **Retest:** NAD+ proxy markers at week 4 if on NMN co-stack

## 5. Monitoring

Pterostilbene shares resveratrol's SIRT1 thesis, so track the same NAD+-axis and lipid/inflammatory markers — especially if it sits in an NMN stack.

| Biomarker | Target | Frequency | Action if off-target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| LDL cholesterol | Within your target | Baseline, 12 wk | Watch trend — pterostilbene has shown LDL effects in some trials |
| hs-CRP | <1.0 mg/L | Baseline, 12 wk | Persistently high → review inflammation drivers |
| Fasting glucose | <100 mg/dL | 3 months | Rising → audit diet and stack |

## 6. Safety, red flags, and contraindications

- **Human-confirmed PK/safety:** Kapetanovic 2011 established tolerability up to 350 mg/day.
- **Lipid signal:** Some human data shows LDL changes at higher doses — monitor a lipid panel if you run it long term.
- **Stilbene class:** Mild platelet effects are plausible; treat like resveratrol around surgery and anticoagulation.

:::redflag title="Do not self-start without clearance"
- **On anticoagulants or antiplatelet therapy** — stilbene platelet effects; physician clearance required
- **Scheduled surgery** — pause stilbenes per your surgeon's perioperative guidance
- **Pregnancy or breastfeeding** — insufficient supplemental-dose safety data
- **On statins or lipid therapy** — monitor LDL and coordinate with your prescriber
:::
