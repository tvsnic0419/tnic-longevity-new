> **TL;DR** — Dietary nitrate from beetroot is a direct, food-based way to raise nitric oxide — the master vasodilator. Human RCTs consistently show lower blood pressure, better endothelial function, and improved exercise economy (you do more work on less oxygen). One of the cleanest levers for the aging vascular and mitochondrial system. ~400–800 mg nitrate/day.

## 1. What dietary nitrate does (and why it matters)

Nitric-oxide production declines with age, and with it goes vascular flexibility and exercise capacity. Beetroot delivers inorganic nitrate that the body converts to nitrite and then NO through a pathway that works *independently* of the age-impaired eNOS enzyme — effectively a back-up NO supply that gets more valuable as you get older. The measured payoffs are lower blood pressure and more efficient muscles.

**Primary hallmarks targeted:** Altered intercellular communication · Mitochondrial dysfunction

## 2. Mechanism

Oral bacteria reduce nitrate to nitrite; tissues then convert nitrite to NO, especially under the low-oxygen conditions where it is needed most. NO relaxes vessels (lower BP, better flow) and improves mitochondrial efficiency in muscle, lowering the oxygen cost of exercise.

## 3. Evidence

Meta-analyses (Siervo and others) show systolic blood-pressure reductions of ~4–5 mmHg — clinically meaningful — and a large body of sports-science RCTs shows improved time-to-exhaustion and exercise economy. Endothelial function improves acutely.

## 4. Dosing

~400–800 mg nitrate/day (a concentrated beetroot shot, or ~500 g of beetroot). For exercise, dose 2–3 hours before. Do **not** use antibacterial mouthwash — it kills the oral bacteria that start the conversion.

## 5. Monitoring

Home blood pressure over 2–4 weeks. For performance, track perceived exertion and endurance. Beeturia (pink urine) is harmless.

## 6. Safety & red flags

Very safe as food. Caution with nitrate if you take nitrate-based heart medications (additive BP drop) — clear it with a clinician. Skip antiseptic mouthwash on dosing days.

## 7. Synergies & stack integration

Stacks with **cocoa flavanols** (NO via two routes) and **L-citrulline** (eNOS substrate) for layered vascular support, and complements any endurance-training protocol.
