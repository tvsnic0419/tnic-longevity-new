> **TL;DR** — Glycine is the "Gly" in [GlyNAC](/library/compounds/glynac): the glycine + cysteine pairing is what restores glutathione in aging humans, because supplying cysteine (NAC) alone isn't enough. Standalone, glycine also has real human data for **sleep quality** (~3 g before bed) and is the most abundant amino acid in collagen. Tier B, cheap, and very safe.

## 1. What glycine does

Glycine is the simplest amino acid, but it sits at three junctions that matter for aging: it is a **rate-contributing substrate for glutathione** (the tripeptide GSH is glutamate-cysteine-glycine), the dominant amino acid in **collagen** (roughly one residue in three), and an **inhibitory neurotransmitter** that improves sleep. The glutathione role is the headline — the GlyNAC thesis is that aging glutathione deficiency needs *both* glycine and cysteine restored, not cysteine alone.

**Primary hallmarks targeted:** [Loss of proteostasis](/library/loss-of-proteostasis) (glutathione substrate) · [Altered intercellular communication](/library/altered-intercellular-communication) (sleep / inhibitory signaling) · [Chronic inflammation](/library/chronic-inflammation)

:::evidence tier="B"
The strongest longevity-relevant data is as half of GlyNAC: the Kumar RCTs restored glutathione, lowered oxidative stress, and improved multiple aging markers in older adults (PMID 34129059). Standalone glycine (~3 g before bed) improved subjective sleep quality and next-day alertness in controlled human studies (Yamadera 2007; Bannai 2012). Tier B — real human data, but glycine's biggest longevity effect is in the glycine+cysteine pairing, not solo.
:::

:::practical title="When glycine is worth it"
Glycine earns a slot if you want the GlyNAC glutathione effect (pair it with NAC), if you have poor sleep onset (~3 g before bed), or as a cheap collagen-synthesis substrate. It's one of the lowest-risk additions in the library — the ceiling on effect size is modest, but so is the cost and downside.
:::

## 3. Evidence summary

| Study | Design | Key outcomes | Tier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GlyNAC (Kumar, PMID 34129059) | Human RCT (glycine + NAC) | Restored GSH, lower oxidative stress, improved aging markers | B |
| Glycine sleep studies (Yamadera 2007; Bannai 2012) | Small human trials | Improved subjective sleep quality, reduced onset latency | B |

**Consensus:** Tier B. Best value as the glycine half of GlyNAC; standalone, a legitimate low-risk sleep and collagen-substrate aid.

## 4. Dosing protocol

- **Glutathione support:** 3 g/day glycine paired with ~600 mg NAC (the GlyNAC ratio from the trials), or use a combined GlyNAC product.
- **Sleep:** ~3 g dissolved in water 30–60 min before bed (mildly sweet).
- **Collagen substrate:** 3–5 g/day; pairs with vitamin C.
- **Duration:** judge sleep within 2 weeks; glutathione effects over 12+ weeks.

## 6. Safety, red flags, and contraindications

- **Very well tolerated** at several grams per day; mild GI upset is the main complaint.
- **Clozapine interaction** — glycine may blunt clozapine's antipsychotic effect.
- **Severe kidney or liver disease** — high amino-acid loads warrant physician oversight.

:::redflag title="Coordinate before starting if any apply"
- **On clozapine** — possible reduced efficacy
- **Severe renal or hepatic disease** — physician oversight for amino-acid loading
- **Pregnancy** — keep to dietary amounts absent clinician guidance
:::
