> **TL;DR** — Bacopa monnieri is a traditional Ayurvedic herb with the most consistent human RCT base of any cognitive-support botanical in this library: multiple placebo-controlled trials (and meta-analyses) show improved memory-formation and recall over 8–12+ weeks of consistent use. The effect is real but slow to appear — this is not an acute nootropic. Tier B; 300 mg/day of a standardized extract (≥50% bacosides).

## 1. What Bacopa does

Bacopa monnieri is a traditional Ayurvedic herb whose active compounds (bacosides) have been studied in a genuinely larger and more consistent set of human RCTs than most botanical nootropics — several independent research groups, multiple standardized extracts, and at least one published meta-analysis pooling the data all point the same direction: improved memory-formation and free-recall performance with **sustained** use.

**Primary hallmarks targeted:** [Altered intercellular communication](/library/altered-intercellular-communication) (cognitive/neuronal function)

:::evidence tier="B"
Multiple placebo-controlled human RCTs (Stough et al. and others, using standardized extracts of ≥50% bacosides) report improved memory-formation and recall performance after 8–12 weeks of daily use, with a meta-analysis-level synthesis supporting the direction and consistency of effect across independent trials. The effect is **cumulative, not acute** — trials consistently show no significant difference at early timepoints (e.g., week 4) with benefit emerging by week 8–12. Tier B: one of the more consistently replicated cognitive botanicals, with realistic timeline expectations.
:::

:::practical title="This is a slow-burn compound — set the right timeline"
Bacopa's own trial literature is unusually consistent on one point: **benefit does not appear at 4 weeks** and emerges by 8–12 weeks of daily, uninterrupted use. If you stop early because "nothing happened," you're stopping before the trials' own timeline for effect.
:::

## 4. Dosing protocol

- **Dose:** 300 mg/day of a standardized extract (≥50% bacosides), matching most positive trials.
- **Timing:** with food (bacosides are better tolerated with meals; GI upset is more common fasted).
- **Duration:** commit to at least 12 weeks before judging — this is the compound's own evidence-based minimum timeline.

## 6. Safety, red flags, and contraindications

- **Generally well tolerated** with food; GI upset (nausea, cramping) is the most common complaint, especially fasted.
- **Thyroid interaction** — some evidence of effects on thyroid hormone; caution if on thyroid medication.
- **Sedative interaction** — mild calming effect reported in some users; caution combining with sedatives.

:::redflag title="Coordinate before starting if any apply"
- **On thyroid medication** — possible interaction; physician coordination advised
- **On sedative medication** — possible additive calming effect
:::
