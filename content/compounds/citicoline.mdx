> **TL;DR** — Citicoline is a highly bioavailable choline source that does double duty: it supplies the raw material for acetylcholine (attention, memory) and for phosphatidylcholine, the phospholipid that builds and repairs neuronal membranes. Human trials show improved attention and memory in healthy adults and support after ischemic injury. A well-tolerated, mechanistically clean cognition tool. 250–500 mg/day.

## 1. What citicoline does (and why it matters)

Two things age the brain's signaling: falling acetylcholine tone and degrading neuronal membranes. Citicoline (CDP-choline) feeds both — it is a direct precursor to the neurotransmitter of focus and memory *and* to the phospholipids that keep synaptic membranes intact. That dual role is why it shows up as attention and memory gains in otherwise healthy people, not just in disease.

**Primary hallmarks targeted:** Altered intercellular communication

## 2. Mechanism

Citicoline is cleaved to cytidine and choline, which are used to synthesize acetylcholine and, via the Kennedy pathway, phosphatidylcholine for membrane synthesis and repair. It also supports dopaminergic tone and mitochondrial function in neurons.

## 3. Evidence

Randomized trials in healthy adults (e.g., McGlade) report improved attention, psychomotor speed, and memory at 250–500 mg/day. It has a large evidence base in ischemic and age-related cognitive decline, with mixed but generally supportive results. Consistent, benign, and fast-acting.

## 4. Dosing

250–500 mg/day, morning or midday. Effects on attention can appear within days; membrane-level benefits accrue over weeks.

## 5. Monitoring

Subjective focus and memory; a brief cognitive test at baseline and 4–8 weeks if you want an objective read. No routine labs.

## 6. Safety & red flags

Excellent safety and tolerability; mild GI upset or headache is uncommon. No significant drug interactions at standard doses.

## 7. Synergies & stack integration

Pairs with **L-theanine** (calm focus) and **lion's mane** (NGF) in a cognition stack, and with **omega-3** for membrane phospholipid supply. Complements **creatine**, which also supports brain energetics.
