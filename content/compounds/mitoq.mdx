> **TL;DR** — MitoQ is CoQ10 re-engineered to actually get *inside* mitochondria — a lipophilic cation drags it hundreds of times more concentrated into the mitochondrial membrane, exactly where age-related oxidative stress does its damage. A landmark human RCT showed it restored vascular function in older adults to that of much younger people. The most targeted mitochondrial antioxidant with real human data. 10–20 mg/day.

## 1. What MitoQ does (and why it matters)

Ordinary antioxidants mostly wash around the cell and barely reach the mitochondrial inner membrane, where the damage that ages tissues concentrates. MitoQ solves the delivery problem: a triphenylphosphonium tail pulls ubiquinol several-hundred-fold into the mitochondria, letting it neutralize reactive oxygen species at the source. That precision is why it produced a striking human vascular result where generic CoQ10 often does not.

**Primary hallmarks targeted:** Mitochondrial dysfunction · Altered intercellular communication

## 2. Mechanism

The lipophilic cation accumulates MitoQ inside the mitochondrial matrix, where it cycles between ubiquinol and ubiquinone forms to quench lipid peroxidation and mitochondrial ROS, preserving membrane integrity and endothelial NO signaling.

## 3. Evidence

A double-blind crossover RCT in healthy older adults (Rossman et al., 2018) found that six weeks of MitoQ improved flow-mediated dilation by ~42%, effectively rejuvenating endothelial function, with reduced oxidative stress markers. Additional trials are exploring exercise and metabolic endpoints.

## 4. Dosing

10–20 mg/day on an empty stomach in the morning. Consistency matters more than dose.

## 5. Monitoring

Blood pressure and, if available, flow-mediated dilation. hs-CRP and general energy/recovery as secondary reads over 6–12 weeks.

## 6. Safety & red flags

Well tolerated in trials; mild GI upset possible at higher doses. Human long-term data is still accumulating, so stay near studied doses and cycle if you prefer. Discuss with a clinician if you are on cardiovascular medication.

## 7. Synergies & stack integration

Complements **PQQ** (build new mitochondria while MitoQ protects them) and **NAD⁺ precursors** (fuel the electron-transport chain), and sits alongside **beetroot nitrate** and **cocoa flavanols** in a vascular-aging stack.
