> **TL;DR** — Nattokinase is a fibrinolytic enzyme purified from natto (fermented soybeans) that directly breaks down fibrin — the protein mesh of blood clots — and supports healthy blood flow. Human RCTs show meaningful reductions in blood pressure and improvements in clot-dissolving activity, with emerging data on arterial plaque. A distinctive, well-tolerated cardiovascular tool. 2,000 FU/day.

## 1. What nattokinase does (and why it matters)

Blood that clots too readily and flows too thickly is a quiet driver of cardiovascular risk that most supplements never touch. Nattokinase is unusual: it is an actual enzyme with direct fibrinolytic activity — it dissolves fibrin the way the body's own plasmin does — and it also boosts the body's clot-dissolving machinery. That gives it a mechanism no antioxidant or polyphenol has, and it shows up as lower blood pressure and better flow in human trials.

**Primary hallmarks targeted:** Altered intercellular communication · Chronic inflammation

## 2. Mechanism

Nattokinase cleaves cross-linked fibrin directly and enhances endogenous fibrinolysis by raising plasmin activity and reducing PAI-1 and fibrinogen. The result is lower blood viscosity, improved microcirculation, and modest blood-pressure reduction via better vascular flow.

## 3. Evidence

A randomized controlled trial (Kim et al., 2008) found 2,000 FU/day lowered systolic and diastolic blood pressure over 8 weeks, and later trials report improved fibrinolytic markers, reduced blood viscosity, and — in a 2022 study — reduced progression of carotid plaque. The fibrinolytic mechanism is well characterized biochemically.

## 4. Dosing

2,000 fibrinolytic units (FU) per day — the studied dose — typically as a single capsule, away from meals. Consistency over weeks matters for the vascular endpoints.

## 5. Monitoring

Home blood pressure over 4–8 weeks. If plaque or clotting is the focus, discuss fibrinogen, D-dimer, or carotid imaging with your clinician at baseline and follow-up.

## 6. Safety & red flags

Well tolerated on its own, but because it is genuinely antithrombotic it should **not** be combined with anticoagulant or antiplatelet drugs without physician oversight, and should be stopped before surgery. Avoid if you have a bleeding disorder or are pregnant.

## 7. Synergies & stack integration

Complements **aged garlic**, **beetroot nitrate**, and **cocoa flavanols** in a cardiovascular stack — each hits a different lever (fibrinolysis, nitric oxide, blood pressure) — and pairs with **omega-3** for blood-flow support. Do not stack with pharmaceutical blood thinners without medical guidance.
