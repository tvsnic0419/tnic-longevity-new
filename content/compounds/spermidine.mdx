> **TL;DR** — Spermidine is a dietary polyamine that induces autophagy via EP300 inhibition. Tier B: Madeo 2021 *Cell* RCT improved memory in older adults; epidemiology links intake to longevity. 1–6 mg supplemental or wheat-germ dietary sources; retest cognition and fasting markers at week 12.

## 1. What spermidine does (and why it matters)

Autophagy — cellular self-recycling — declines with age. Spermidine reactivates cleanup pathways without requiring extended fasting, making it a mechanistic complement to mTOR-modulating lifestyle and NAD+ restoration.

**Primary hallmarks targeted:** [Disabled autophagy](/library/disabled-autophagy) · [Epigenetic alterations](/library/epigenetic-alterations) · [Cellular senescence](/library/cellular-senescence)

:::evidence tier="B"
PMID 33932338 — Spermidine supplementation improves memory performance and mitochondrial function in older adults (*Cell Reports Medicine* / Madeo group, 2021). PMID 30093609 — mechanistic autophagy review (*Science*).
:::

## 3. Evidence summary

| Study | Design | Key outcomes | Tier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Madeo 2021 (PMID 33932338) | Human RCT | Memory improvement, mitochondrial markers | B |
| Kiechl 2018, *Neurology* (PMID 29514097) | 829-person 20-yr cohort | Highest dietary spermidine tertile: HR 0.60 all-cause mortality, HR 0.40 cardiovascular mortality vs. lowest tertile | B |

**Consensus:** Best human data is cognitive/metabolic pilot outcomes — not lifespan endpoints. Tier B autophagy adjunct.

## 4. Dosing protocol

- **Supplement:** 1–6 mg spermidine-rich extract daily
- **Dietary:** Wheat germ, aged cheese, legumes (variable content)
- **Pair with:** PM resveratrol or fasting windows for autophagy synergy
- **Retest:** Cognitive self-assessment + optional fasting glucose at week 12

:::decision title="Should you start spermidine?"
node | Autophagy/proteostasis is a top hallmark priority and you already run TRE or Zone 2?
yes | Add 1–3 mg/day with a 12-week cognitive + fasting-glucose readout
no | Build the lifestyle foundation first — spermidine supports fasting, it does not replace it
redflag | On immunosuppressants or active infection → physician clearance before inducing autophagy
:::

## 5. Monitoring

Spermidine's mechanism (autophagy induction) has no cheap direct assay, so track the downstream cognitive and metabolic signals its human trials measured.

| Biomarker | Target | Frequency | Action if off-target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cognitive self-assessment | Stable or improving | Baseline, 12 wk | No change → confirm dose form and adherence |
| Fasting glucose | <100 mg/dL | 3 months | Rising → audit diet before adjusting spermidine |
| hs-CRP | <1.0 mg/L | Baseline, 12 wk | Persistently high → review inflammation drivers |
| Sleep quality | ≥7/10 | Daily log | Autophagy benefits assume adequate sleep — fix this first |

## 6. Safety, red flags, and contraindications

- **Food-derived and well tolerated:** Supplemental doses (1–6 mg) are far below dietary intakes in high-polyamine populations.
- **Autophagy is context-dependent:** Inducing cellular recycling is not universally desirable during active infection or immunosuppression.

:::redflag title="Do not self-start without clearance"
- **On immunosuppressants** (transplant, autoimmune therapy) — autophagy modulation interacts with treatment intent; physician review required
- **Active infection or cancer treatment** — coordinate autophagy-inducing supplements with your care team
- **Pregnancy or breastfeeding** — insufficient supplemental-dose safety data
:::
