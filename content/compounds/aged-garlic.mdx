> **TL;DR** — Aged garlic extract is one of the best-evidenced natural cardiovascular supplements: human RCTs show meaningful blood-pressure reduction, and imaging trials show it slows the progression of coronary artery plaque. The aging process converts harsh allicin into stable, bioavailable S-allylcysteine — so it delivers the benefit without the burn or the breath. 600–1,200 mg/day.

## 1. What aged garlic extract does (and why it matters)

Blood pressure and arterial plaque are two of the highest-leverage targets in cardiovascular aging, and aged garlic extract (AGE) has real human data on both. Beyond lowering BP, imaging trials show it actually slows coronary calcium and soft-plaque progression — an outcome few supplements can claim. The aging step (up to 20 months) trades unstable allicin for stable, water-soluble S-allylcysteine, giving reliable dosing and none of the GI harshness of raw garlic.

**Primary hallmarks targeted:** Altered intercellular communication · Chronic inflammation · Deregulated nutrient sensing

## 2. Mechanism

S-allylcysteine and related compounds raise hydrogen-sulfide and nitric-oxide signaling (vasodilation, lower BP), inhibit LDL oxidation and platelet aggregation, and blunt vascular inflammation — a multi-pronged effect on the drivers of atherosclerosis.

## 3. Evidence

Meta-analyses (Ried) show systolic BP reductions comparable to first-line lifestyle changes, and Budoff's imaging RCTs show slowed coronary plaque progression and improvements in vascular calcium scoring over 12 months. Modest cholesterol effects add to the picture.

## 4. Dosing

600–1,200 mg/day of a standardized aged garlic extract, split with meals. Benefits accrue over 3–12 months, especially for the plaque endpoint.

## 5. Monitoring

Home blood pressure over 4–8 weeks; lipid panel and, if plaque is the goal, a coronary calcium score at baseline and 12 months.

## 6. Safety & red flags

Very well tolerated (aged extract avoids most GI upset). It has mild antiplatelet activity, so tell your clinician before surgery or if you take anticoagulants/antiplatelets.

## 7. Synergies & stack integration

Layers with **beetroot nitrate** and **cocoa flavanols** for blood pressure and NO, and with **omega-3** and **bergamot** for a full cardiovascular stack targeting BP, lipids, and plaque together.
