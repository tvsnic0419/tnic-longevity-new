> **TL;DR** — NR is a legitimate NAD+ precursor with human RCT data. TNiC standardizes on NMN for stack integration, but NR is defensible if your physician prefers it or you already respond. Never combine high-dose NR + NMN without oversight.

## 1. NR vs NMN — same destination, different molecule

Both nicotinamide riboside (NR) and NMN feed the NAD+ salvage pathway:

```
NR → NMN → NAD+ → SIRT1/3, PARP, CD38 balance
```

NR requires one enzymatic step (NRK) before becoming NMN. In practice, **both raise NAD+ metabolites in humans** — the debate is dose economics, stack integration, and individual response — not whether NAD+ restoration works.

:::evidence tier="A"
Martens 2018 (PMID 29599478): NR elevated whole-blood NAD+ in healthy middle-aged and older adults (Nature Communications RCT). Trammell 2016 and subsequent RCTs confirm human PK. Compare head-to-head in [NMN vs NR](/library/compare/nmn-vs-nr).
:::

## 3. Evidence summary

| Study | Molecule | Dose | Outcome | Tier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Martens 2018 | NR | 1000 mg | ↑ whole-blood NAD+ | A |
| Trammell 2016 | NR | 100–1000 mg | ↑ NAD+ metabolites | A |
| Igarashi 2022 | NMN | 250 mg | ↑ NAD+, functional signal | A |

**Honest framing:** Tier A for NAD+ elevation. No human longevity endpoint trial for either precursor. Mouse data supports both.

## 6. Monitoring

NR and NMN are judged on the same readout: does whole-blood NAD+ actually rise, and does function follow?

| Biomarker | Target | Frequency | Action if off-target |

| NAD+ index | 80–100 | Baseline, 4 wk, 12 wk | No rise by week 4 → verify product identity (NR-Cl) and dose |

## 7. Safety, red flags, and contraindications

- **Well tolerated:** Human RCTs (Martens 2018, Trammell 2016) report good tolerability at 300–1000 mg/day.
- **Do not double-dose precursors:** Combining high-dose NR **and** NMN offers no proven additive benefit and needs physician oversight.
- **Methyl donor drain:** Long-term high-dose NAD+ precursors can increase nicotinamide methylation — ensure adequate B12, folate, and betaine on 6+ month protocols.

:::redflag title="Do not self-start without clearance"
- **Active chemotherapy or immunotherapy** — NAD+ biology interacts with treatment intent; oncologist clearance required
- **Pregnancy or breastfeeding** — insufficient human safety data
- **Combining 500 mg+ NR with 500 mg+ NMN** — physician oversight required; do not stack precursors casually
- **Unexplained muscle pain worsening on NR** — pause and rule out metabolic causes
:::
