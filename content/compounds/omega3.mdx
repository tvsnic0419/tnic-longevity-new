Tier A across cardiovascular, inflammatory, and telomere endpoints. 2–4 g combined EPA+DHA with food; retest omega-3 index, hs-CRP, and triglycerides at week 12.

## 1. What omega-3 does (and why it matters)

Most anti-inflammatory strategies suppress signaling; EPA and DHA do something rarer — they fuel the *resolution* phase of inflammation through specialized pro-resolving mediators (resolvins, protectins, maresins). This active clean-up is central to healthy aging, because chronic unresolved inflammation ("inflammaging") drives most age-related disease. Omega-3s also support telomere maintenance and cardiovascular structure, giving them one of the broadest evidence bases in longevity nutrition.

**Primary hallmarks targeted:** Chronic inflammation · Altered intercellular communication · Telomere attrition · Dysbiosis

:::evidence tier="A"
PMID 30345336 — REDUCE-IT (*NEJM*, 2019). 4 g/day icosapent ethyl (EPA) reduced major adverse cardiovascular events 25% in high-risk statin-treated patients — the definitive omega-3 cardiovascular outcome trial. Telomere data: PMID 22245710 (*Brain, Behavior, and Immunity*, 2012).
:::

## 2. Mechanism — resolution, not suppression

EPA and DHA are enzymatically converted to SPMs that signal immune cells to stop recruiting, clear debris, and return tissue to homeostasis. This is mechanistically distinct from NSAID-style suppression and explains omega-3's durable cardiovascular and inflammatory benefits. DHA additionally supports neuronal membrane fluidity and telomerase-associated pathways.

| --- | --- | --- |
| SPM resolution | EPA/DHA → resolvins, protectins, maresins | Chronic inflammation |
| Cardiovascular | Lowers triglycerides, stabilizes plaque | Altered intercellular communication |
| Telomere support | Higher omega-3 index correlates with slower attrition | Telomere attrition |

:::practical title="When omega-3 is worth your money"
Omega-3 earns a stack slot when **two or more** apply:

- Low oily-fish intake (fewer than 2 servings/week)
- Elevated triglycerides or hs-CRP
- A low omega-3 index (<8%) on testing

**Skip or defer** high doses if you take anticoagulants without physician oversight — omega-3s have mild antiplatelet effects.
:::

## 3. Evidence summary

| Study | Design | Key outcomes | Tier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| REDUCE-IT (PMID 30345336) | RCT, 8,179 patients | Major cardiac events −25% at 4 g EPA/day | A |
| Kiecolt-Glaser 2012 (PMID 22245710) | RCT, healthy adults | Higher omega-3 → longer leukocyte telomeres | A |

**Consensus:** Tier A for cardiovascular and inflammatory endpoints — the outcome data are large and consistent. The resolution-of-inflammation mechanism and telomere associations make it one of the best-evidenced longevity nutrients, though dose and form (EPA-dominant, high-dose) matter for the cardiovascular effect.

:::

## 4. Dosing protocol

- **Start:** 2 g combined EPA+DHA/day with a meal
- **Target:** 2–4 g/day; the cardiovascular RCT used 4 g EPA-dominant
- **Form:** Re-esterified triglyceride (rTG) absorbs best; ethyl-ester (EE) is more oxidation-prone — verify total EPA+DHA mg, not just "fish oil" mg
- **Timing:** With food; refrigerate to limit rancidity
- **Duration:** 12 weeks before rechecking omega-3 index and triglycerides

## 6. Safety, red flags, and contraindications

- **Well tolerated:** Fishy reflux is the most common complaint — mitigated by rTG form, refrigeration, and taking with food.
- **Antiplatelet effect:** High doses mildly reduce platelet aggregation; relevant before surgery or on anticoagulants.
- **Oxidation:** Rancid oil is pro-inflammatory — buy fresh, IFOS-tested product and store cold.

:::redflag title="Do not self-start high doses without clearance"
- **On anticoagulants or antiplatelet drugs** — additive bleeding risk; physician coordination
- **Before scheduled surgery** — pause high-dose per your surgeon's guidance
- **Fish/shellfish allergy** — use algal (vegan) DHA/EPA instead
- **Atrial fibrillation history** — very high doses have been associated with AF in some trials; discuss with cardiology
:::
